Built during a student internship at NIT Warangal. Doctors and patients get separate dashboards, and appointment state lives on-chain in a Solidity `HealthSystem` contract.

## Highlights

- Smart contract with patient, doctor and account structures and a meeting lifecycle: create, accept and reject appointments
- React (Vite) front end that connects to MetaMask through Web3.js
- Express and MongoDB server for off-chain data, with doctor and patient views and file upload screens
